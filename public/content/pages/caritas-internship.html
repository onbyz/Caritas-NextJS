Overview

 

Caritas Internship/Observership Program has been pivotal in developing skills for medical professionals especially for students from overseas universities.

This is an observership program with no hands-on clinical exposure.

 

Eligibility :

 

  • Candidates pursuing MBBS degree overseas
  • Candidates who completed MBBS program and wants to improve their clinical knowledge
  • Fees applicable
